Signature Architecture

Your recorder never ruins your recording.

RecordFlow registers its main workspace, draggable Floating Recording Control Pill, and Presenter Script Teleprompter with Windows WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE. Drag the mini Stop/Pause pill to any corner on your screen and Windows Graphics Capture skips it completely.

  • Pause or stop from any app using the always-on-top draggable mini pill — invisible in your recorded video.
  • Read your always-on-top Presenter Teleprompter straight over your app while viewers see only clean footage.
  • Zero awkward post-trimming to cut off "Start / Stop" button clicks.

Complete Studio Capabilities

Everything to capture, polish, and ship — all in one local app.

Rebuilt from the ground up in v1.3.5 to combine native Windows capture speed with a non-destructive presentation editor.

Screen, Area & Isolated Window Capture

Record full displays, draw a translucent pixel-exact capture rectangle with 8 resize handles and live pixel dimensions, or pick any active Windows app with real-time PrintWindow thumbnails so only that window is recorded.

Multi-monitor 8-handle Area Picker PrintWindow Previews

60 Hz Cursor Tracking & One-Click Auto-Zoom

A Win32 background sampler logs your mouse path and clicks at 60 Hz into .cursor.json. In the Edit Studio, generate cinematic smooth zoom keyframes automatically, adjust Subtle / Balanced / Close intensity, or add cursor click ripples and spotlight halos.

60 Hz Sampler Click-to-Aim Ripples & Halo

Independent Webcam Layer

Captured via nokhwa into its own synchronized .cam.mp4 track. Move, scale, mirror, reshape (Circle, Rounded, Square), or hide your webcam after recording.

.cam.mp4 Non-destructive

Post-Recording Presentation Studio

Frame recordings for YouTube (16:9), Reels/Shorts (9:16), 1:1, or 4:5 with 18 curated backgrounds, custom wallpaper uploads, draggable privacy blur masks, and waveform timeline trimming.

18 Backgrounds Privacy Blur

WASAPI System Audio + Mic Mixer

Record crisp microphone voiceovers alongside Windows WASAPI loopback system audio via cpal, then balance mic, system, and master levels with smooth fade-in and fade-out.
